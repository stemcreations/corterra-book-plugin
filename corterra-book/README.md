# Corterra Book

Run your Corterra Book booking business from Claude or ChatGPT. This plugin connects the AI
app to your own Corterra Book account and adds eight skills, step-by-step guides the AI
follows for the jobs owners ask for most:

- **set-up-my-business**: set up a new business, or see what is already set up
- **book-and-reschedule**: book, move, cancel, confirm and check in appointments
- **fill-quiet-days**: find quiet days and invite the regulars who usually book them
- **win-back-clients**: find customers who stopped coming and decide how to reach them
- **services-and-products**: add or change services, classes, add-ons and retail items
- **memberships-and-packs**: add or change memberships, packs and combos, and what the online shop sells
- **hours-and-closures**: change your hours, close for a day or part of a day, set holidays
- **weekly-review**: last week's bookings, new clients, no-shows and sales, and next week's gaps

## How to use it

1. Install the plugin, then connect Corterra Book from its Connectors tab.
2. Sign in with your Corterra Book dashboard email and password, choose **Read & write**,
   check the business name, and press **Allow**. Only the business owner can connect.
3. Ask in your own words, for example "Help me set up my business" or "How did last week
   look?".

The AI asks before it saves anything. It can never charge a card, take a payment or issue a
refund.

## What data it sends

The AI app reads and changes your business's information through your Corterra Book
account when you ask it to: your settings, services, hours, customers, bookings and, if you
allow it, sales figures. The app provider handles what it receives under its own privacy
policy. Corterra Book's privacy policy: https://corterrabook.com/legal/privacy. Disconnect
any time from Add-ons, then AI Assistant, in your dashboard.
